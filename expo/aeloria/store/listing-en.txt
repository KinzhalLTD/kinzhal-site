The Sunspire Crown is shattered, and the Hollow King's ash is spreading across Aeloria. Choose your home realm, raise a citadel and gather heroes from every corner of the world to take the throne back.

BUILD A PAINTED CITADEL
Grow farms, lumber camps, quarries and mines. Upgrade the Hall of Heroes, Barracks, Academy, Embassy and towering Ramparts. Watch your kingdom come alive as it grows.

LEAD LEGENDARY HEROES
Recruit 21 heroes: original champions from six realms and great figures of history and legend, including King Arthur, Antarah ibn Shaddad, Zarqa al-Yamama, Sun Tzu, El Cid and Leonardo da Vinci. Level them up, raise their stars and build your perfect squad.

CINEMATIC BATTLES
Command painted armies of infantry, archers, cavalry and siege engines. Time each hero's ultimate for a perfect strike, break enemy charges and topple giant bosses.

AN EPIC SIX-CHAPTER STORY
Journey from green valleys to silent fjords, coral seas and golden dunes, all the way to the Sunspire through illustrated story scenes and cinematic chapter openings.

EXPLORE A VAST WORLD
Scout a fog-covered continent, find treasure and merchants, conquer outposts and hunt Elite Warbands for rich rewards.

GROW WITHOUT LIMITS
Research technologies, train elite troops, forge endless Royal Forge upgrades, complete Daily Deeds and climb the Season Road.
