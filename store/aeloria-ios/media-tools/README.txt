AELORIA: AGE OF CROWNS: APP STORE SCREENSHOTS AND APP PREVIEWS
==============================================================

Every image and video is captured from the real game, as Apple requires, at the exact pixel sizes App Store Connect accepts.
Upload in the numbered order 01 to 08. The first three appear in search results.

WHERE TO UPLOAD
App Store Connect → your app → iOS App 1.0 → choose the language at the top right → "Previews and Screenshots".
Pick the device size tab, then drag in the files.

REQUIRED (Apple will not let you submit without these)
  iPhone 6.9" display .... screenshots/<language>/iPhone-6.9in_1320x2868/
  iPad 13" display ....... screenshots/<language>/iPad-13in_2064x2752/      (the app supports iPad)

App Store Connect scales those two sets down for every smaller iPhone and iPad. The other folders are optional.
They give a sharper, exact-fit result in the size tabs App Store Connect offers ("View all sizes in Media Manager").
